Notes

  • Single knife-edge: free-space loss plus J(ν) of the dominant edge, ν from the largest Fresnel–Kirchhoff parameter along the path with effective-Earth curvature (ITU-R P.526).
  • Delta-Bullington: Bullington construction on the actual profile, corrected by the spherical-Earth loss minus Bullington on a smooth profile (ITU-R P.526-16 §4.5, as used in P.1812-8 §4.3). Median loss, using the k entered.
  • ITU-R P.1812-8: full method (line-of-sight with multipath/focusing, delta-Bullington diffraction, troposcatter, ducting/layer reflection), time % and location %. ΔN and N0 come from the ITU maps. Validated against the ITU-R SG3/Ofcom test set (land paths). Land paths only in this tool.
  • Control points: line of sight shows the dominant edge. Beyond line of sight shows the TX and RX horizon obstacles and the Bullington point where the two horizon rays meet.
  • Fresnel: clearance is reported as the smallest (ray − surface) / r1 along the path. ≥ 60 % of the first zone is the usual “clear” criterion.
  • Grid: enter an OS National Grid reference (e.g. SK 570 395) or a Maidenhead locator (e.g. IO92KW). Placing a site fills both forms.
  • Planning aid only. Verify critical links with measurements.
